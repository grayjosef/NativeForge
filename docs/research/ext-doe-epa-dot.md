### A5. EPA — GAP (the single most important EPA row; **fully verified from the FY26 NOFA PDF**)

Fetched `epa.gov/general-assistance-program-gap` (modified 2026-08-19) and the authoritative **FY26 NOFA PDF** (`epa.gov/system/files/documents/2025-11/final-fy26-gap-nofa.pdf`, released 2025-11-14).

- **ALNs (verified, verbatim):** GAP = **66.926**, Funding Opportunity Number **EPA-CEP-02**. Performance Partnership Grants = **66.605**, FON **EPA-CEP-01**.
- **Eligibility (verified, and restrictive):** "Only these types of organizations may apply… **Federally recognized Indian Tribal government. Intertribal consortia.**" Per 40 CFR 35.502 the definition requires an entity "recognized as eligible by the U.S. Department of the Interior for the special services provided by the United States to Indians because of their status as Indians." **Federal recognition is required. State-recognized Tribes are excluded — there is no alternate pathway in this NOFA.** Intertribal consortia are "the only type of coalition eligible to apply," and require majority-GAP-eligible membership plus **unanimous** authorizing resolutions ("no abstentions and no majority approvals").
- **Not competitive; regionally allocated.** ~**520+ awards** anticipated. Funding order: individual Tribes first, then intertribal consortia. Floor **$75,000** for initial awards; ceiling 10% of the total FY26 appropriation. Project periods up to **4 years** (5 in a PPG). **No cost share** (stand-alone or in a PPG); PPG class exception effective 10/1/2021.
- **Deadline pattern is per-EPA-Region, not national — this is a real product requirement.** Verified FY26 dates: R1 = 45 days from allocation letter (Grants.gov **July 3, 2026**); R2 = Feb/March 2026; R3 = **May 29, 2026**; R4 = **April 17, 2026** (Grants.gov June 19, 2026); R5 = 60 days from allocation letter; R6 = **February 27, 2026**; R7 = Tribe-specific; R8 = **February 26, 2026** (Grants.gov June 2, 2026); R9 = **February 6, 2026**; R10 = **February 13, 2026** (Grants.gov May 22, 2026). The recurring pattern is **Feb–July, region-dependent**, with the national NOFA published the preceding **November**. Award notification "no later than September 30, 2026."
- **Software / data systems / planning / admin allowability: `clearly-allowable`.** Direct supporting language: GAP "supports Tribal government efforts to develop core environmental program capacities, such as **administrative, financial management, information management**, environmental baseline needs assessment, public education/communication, legal, technical/analytical, and capacity needed to implement and/or meaningfully participate in federal environmental protection programs." Budget categories expressly include "personnel, fringe, travel, equipment, supplies, **contracts, consultants**, other, and indirect costs." Tribes may claim a **15% de minimis indirect rate** (2 CFR 200.414(f)). Constraint to encode: costs must map to an approved **work plan component** and to a priority in the Tribe's **ETEP**; if no ETEP exists, developing one must be a work plan commitment.
- **Adjacent systems worth tracking:** EPA's **GAP Hub** (AIEO data-management application; 110 ETEPs loaded as of the Summer 2025 Innovations report) and **GAP Capacity Indicators** — direct evidence EPA itself is buying/building the category of tooling NativeForge sells.

### A6. EPA — Water (verified-active, strong detail)

- **CWA §106 Tribal Grants** (`epa.gov/water-pollution-control-section-106-grants/tribal-grants-under-section-106-clean-water-act`, modified 2026-03-24). **Formula, non-competitive, allocated to the nine Regions with federally recognized Tribes** (R3 has none with TAS): base allotment **$65,167 per TAS-eligible Tribe** plus a variable allotment on land area, population, and surface water area. Tribes apply to the **Regional office** with a work plan and budget. **Requires federal recognition AND TAS approval** — a two-gate eligibility model (~330 of the federally recognized Tribes meet the five TAS requirements; ~75% of those have TAS for §106). Annual tribal set-aside "more than $25 million." **Match waived** by class exception since 4/5/2022 (40 CFR 35.585). **Software allowability: `clearly-allowable`** — verbatim eligible use: "**Developing water quality and geographic information system databases to track changes in water quality and ensure consistency in data management**," plus "hiring program staff and purchasing equipment and supplies." Hard exclusion: no construction/O&M of waste treatment plants, and no costs financed by other federal grants.
- **CWA §319 Tribal — base grants** (`epa.gov/nps/current-tribal-section-319-grant-information`, canonical; modified **2026-08-25**). **Noncompetitive, formula by tribal land area** (FY23+ schedule): <2 sq mi = $50,000 (69 grantees in FY25); 2–50 = $55,000 (71); 50–500 = $60,000 (48); 500+ = $75,000 (31). **Requires TAS + an EPA-approved NPS assessment report and NPS management program.** Work plan deadlines **vary by Region** (contact Region). **Allowability: `likely-allowable`** — eligible activities include "hiring a program coordinator… updating the NPS management program; **developing watershed-based plans**"; software is not named.
- **CWA §319 Tribal — competitive (OPEN RIGHT NOW).** FY26 NOFO **`epa-ow-owow-26-01`**, posted **2026-08-17**, on **Grants.gov opportunity 363611**. **Application deadline Monday, November 9, 2026, 11:59 pm ET** via Grants.gov. Cap **$175,000** federal per tribal applicant; ~**$3.5M/yr** total. Eligible: 319-eligible Tribes **and intertribal consortia**. Informational webinars 9/9/2026 and 9/17/2026; question deadline 10/3/2026; selections announced early spring 2027. **New Applicant Set-Aside** (~5 projects) reserved for applicants not awarded FY2021–FY2025. **Recurring pattern: NOFO posts ~August, closes ~November, selections ~spring.** Grantees must submit project data to **GRTS** within 90 days of award (Excel form or GRTS account) — a compliance workflow NativeForge could serve.
- **CWISA (Clean Water Indian Set-Aside)** — verified-active; administered with **IHS**; "to be considered for CWISA funding, tribes must identify their wastewater needs through the **IHS Sanitation Deficiency System**" — i.e. the real intake is an IHS system, not an EPA competition. FY2025 allotment memo published 2025-05-30.
- **DWIG-TSA** — verified-active. FY2025 tribal infrastructure total announced: **over $262 million** for drinking water and wastewater for Tribes and Alaska Native Villages. FY25 allotment memo 2025-06-12; **FY25 Lead Service Line Replacement allotments memo dated 2025-11-24**. Eligible: community water systems and non-profit non-community water systems serving a tribal population.
- Also verified on the tribal water funding pages: **Tribal PWSS**, **UIC tribal grants** (both keyed to primary enforcement responsibility), **WIIN Act §2104 / §2105 / §2107 tribal grant programs**, **Wetland Program Development Grants** (state/tribal/interstate-intertribal), **BEACH Act grants**, **Alaska Native Villages and Rural Communities Grant Program** (note: **flows through the Alaska DEC Village Safe Water Program** — a state pass-through), and **WIFIA** loans.
- **SRF tribal set-asides generally:** not separately verified this pass beyond DWIG-TSA and CWISA. Mark UNKNOWN.

### A12. DOT — FHWA Office of Tribal Transportation (TTP): the strongest DOT cluster

- Canonical namespace **moved**: `highways.dot.gov/federal-lands/programs-tribal` **301s to `/federal-lands/tribal`**. Both forms still appear as in-page links (the page links its own children under *both* `/programs-tribal/...` and `/tribal/...`) — **store canonical `/federal-lands/tribal/...` and treat `/programs-tribal/...` as legacy aliases.**
- **TTP formula (verified):** 23 U.S.C. 202. OTT "provides stewardship and oversight for direct funding agreements with **135 federally recognized Tribes**." Funded by contract authority from the Highway Trust Fund, subject to the obligation limitation. "Funds are allocated among Tribes using a **statutory formula** based on tribal population, road mileage and average tribal shares of the former Tribal Transportation Allocation Methodology (TTAM)." Governing rule: **25 CFR 170** (final rule 2016-11-07, compliance-date delay 2018-02-28). **Federal recognition is required** — the page links the Federal Register list of "Indian Entities Recognized and Eligible To Receive Services From the… Bureau of Indian Affairs" as the eligibility authority. **No state-recognized pathway.** Non-formula distribution runs to BIA for Tribes that have not taken a direct FHWA agreement.
- **Tribal shares / planning funds (verified):** `/federal-lands/tribal/finance` (updated 2025-12-02) publishes **"Tribal Shares and Planning Funds under IIJA"** as annual PDFs (FY25, FY24 revised, FY23, FY22) plus FAST-Act-era FY17–FY21. **PDF-only; Excel available only by emailing `matthew.bird@dot.gov`.** This is a **PDF/NOFO-page monitor** with high extraction value (per-Tribe dollar amounts) and medium-high parsing difficulty.
- **§202(a)(9) transfers — the state pass-through mechanism, and it is real but small.** Verified: §202(a)(9) "allow[s] any funds received from a State, county, or local government to be credited to appropriations available for the Tribal Transportation Program," used with §104(f)(3) to move a State's title-23 apportionment to FHWA and then to a specified Tribe. **"To date, FHWA has managed approximately 20 transfers across 8 different states."** Templates and procedures are published as PDFs. This is the concrete answer to "how do state DOT funds reach Tribes" on the highway side — low volume, agreement-driven, **not** a posted opportunity. Monitoring method: human review / document watch.
- **TTP Safety Fund (TTPSF) — verified-active, clean annual cycle.** `/federal-lands/tribal/safety/funds`, last updated **2026-03-19**. **4% of available TTP funds set aside each year under IIJA**, awarded through a "competitive, discretionary program" to **federally recognized Tribes only** (links the Federal Register recognition list). Verified cycle: **FY2025 awards announced 2026-03-19**; **2026 TTPSF deadline was January 15, 2026** and "FHWA is evaluating these applications." Operating NOFO is a **multi-year document**: "Notice of Funding Opportunity: **2022–2026** Tribal Transportation Program Safety Fund — **Amendment No. 2**" (PDF hosted at `/federal-lands/tribal/safety/funds/TTPSF_NOFO_Amendment_2_for_publication_clean.pdf`). **Recurring pattern: ~mid-January deadline, awards announced ~March.** Applications are handled by **emailing `TTPSF@dot.gov` for an upload link** — not Grants.gov. Because the NOFO spans 2022–2026, the *amendments* are what change; monitor the PDF for new amendment numbers.
- **TTP Bridge Program:** verified as an OTT-managed **set-aside from the overall TTP** "for all federally recognized Tribes," with pages at `/federal-lands/tribal/bridge` (+ BIA/Tribal Bridge Inspection Program, GRS-IBS, Bridge Q&A). Amounts/deadlines not on the pages I fetched → UNKNOWN.
- **Also verified as OTT monitorable assets:** **Tribal High Priority Projects (TTP HPP)** program page, TTP Coordinating Committee, TTP Annual Report, **TTAP** (Tribal Technical Assistance Program), TTP Planning + **TTIP/STIP** + **e-TTIP** (electronic TTIP — a live government data system for tribal transportation programming), TTP Environment, Surplus & Excess Property, TTP Webinars, and OTT's **Newsletter** page.

### A13. DOT — FTA Tribal Transit (verified-active; best-documented eligibility model in the whole set)

- **Tribal Transit Competitive Program, 49 U.S.C. 5311(c)(1)(A)** (`transit.dot.gov/tribal-transit`). **OPEN AS OF TODAY — and closing today or yesterday.** Verified: "On **May 27, 2026**, FTA announced approximately **$19 million** in available funding for the Fiscal Year 2026 Tribal Transit. **Applications are due by August 25, 2026.**" (Today is 2026-08-26 — treat as just-closed and confirm.) FY26 NOFO page: `transit.dot.gov/notices-funding/fy-2026-notice-funding-opportunity-public-transportation-indian-reservations`. Webinar held 6/17/2026.
- **Structure (verified):** TTP is a **5% takedown from Section 5311** Rural Formula; **20% of TTP must be competitive, the remainder by formula**. Program-level figures: "approximately **$36 million formula** program and a **$9 million competitive** grant program subject to the availability of appropriations." **No local match required on either the formula or the competitive program** (voluntary match permitted). Competitive funds available for **3 fiscal years** (year of apportionment + 2). Award history is published per-FY back to FY2014 ($9.17M FY24, $9.91M FY23, $8.64M FY22, $10.36M FY21…).
- **Formula program gate (verified, and it's a hard one):** "To access TTP formula program funds, prospective recipients **must have reported to the National Transit Database (NTD)** in the most recent NTD report year at time of apportionment.
