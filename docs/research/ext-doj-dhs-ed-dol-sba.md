# NativeForge Source Registry — DOJ, DHS/FEMA, ED, DOL, SBA
**Research date: 2026-08-26.** All statuses reflect page content as fetched on that date only.

## PART A — Per-Agency Narrative

### 1. DOJ

**Structural change — highest-priority signal.** `justice.gov/tribal` carries an active consultation notice: *"the Department's plan to consolidate the Office of Community Oriented Policing Services, the Office of Justice Programs, and the Office on Violence Against Women into a single grantmaking component named the Bureau of Justice Grants."* Virtual sessions Aug 5–6, 2026. **Implication for the scraper map: COPS/OJP/OVW URL structures and NOFO publishing patterns are likely to change.** Build DOJ collectors with an abstraction layer.

**OJP funding page rendering.** `ojp.gov/funding/explore/current-funding-opportunities` is **server-rendered Drupal 10** — full opportunity list, titles, links, and dual deadlines are in the initial HTML. No JS app, no JSON/XHR endpoint observed. Bureau-level lists (`bja.ojp.gov/funding/current`, `ovc.ojp.gov/funding/current-funding-opportunities`, `ojjdp.ojp.gov/funding/current`) are the same CMS. Individual opportunity pages are also server-rendered with a clean field block (Opportunity ID, Solicitation Status, Fiscal Year, Closing Date, Posting Date, Solicitation Type, both deadlines) plus a stable NOFO PDF at `ojp.gov/funding/docs/<opp-id>.pdf`. **No search/filter API endpoint was found.** Page footers carry a `Date Modified` field — a cheap change-detection key. Subscribe endpoints exist (`ojp.gov/subscribe`, `bja.ojp.gov/subscribe`); GovDelivery for DOJ-wide at `public.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDOJ/subscriber/new`, OVW topic `USDOJ_59`, COPS at `service.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDOJCOPS/...`.

**CTAS FY26 — verified.** Opportunity `O-BJA-2026-172662`. Posted 2026-07-24. Competitive. Grants.gov 2026-10-15 23:59 ET; JustGrants 2026-10-22 20:59 ET. Annual FY cycle. ALNs listed on the NOFO cover: **16.710, 16.596, 16.583, 16.585, 16.731, 16.043**. Eligibility, verbatim: *"support federally recognized tribes and tribal consortia"*; applicant list is *"Native American Tribal governments (federally recognized)"* and *"Consortia consisting of two or more federally recognized Indian Tribes."* **State-recognized Tribes are excluded.** FY26 purpose areas (note: **no PA 7 in FY26**):
- PA 1 Public Safety and Community Policing / Tribal Resources Grant Program (COPS), ALN 16.710
- PA 2 Comprehensive Tribal Justice Systems Strategic Planning (BJA)
- PA 3 Tribal Justice Systems Program (BJA), ALN 16.596
- PA 4 Tribal Justice System Infrastructure Program (BJA)
- PA 5 Adult Tribal Treatment Courts (BJA), ALN 16.585, 16.043
- PA 6 Children's Justice Act Partnerships for Indian Communities (OVC)
- PA 8 Enhancing Youth Healing to Wellness Courts (OJJDP)
- PA 9 Tribal Youth Justice Program (OJJDP)

**CTAS cost allowability — the load-bearing detail.** This varies *by purpose area*, and NativeForge should not treat CTAS as one policy:
- **PA 1 (COPS/TRGP): clearly-allowable.** Allowable equipment list includes *"Computer hardware and software, mobile data terminals, radios."* Indirect costs allowable under PA 1.
- **PA 5 (treatment courts): clearly-allowable.** *"Computer hardware and software for internet access and email capability"* and *"Data management systems for record keeping and case management."* Same software line appears under PA 8 and PA 9.
- **PA 2 (strategic planning): sometimes-allowable-depends-on-NOFO, with an express bar on outside planners.** *"No equipment should be budgeted for this project. Costs for office equipment, computing devices, or information technology such as computers, printers, scanners, and tools to support data collection and analysis should be included in the 'Supplies' or 'Other' category."* But: *"Costs for outside TTA providers, planners, or outside organizations to create a strategic plan for the tribe are not an allowable expense."* PA 2 is once-per-tribe; prior awardees ineligible.
- **PA 8/PA 9: consultant bar.** PA 8 — *"Funds are not allowed for additional consultants"*; *"Applicants should not include requests for funding to support on-site consultants as this will be managed by the existing TTA provider."* Software itself is still listed as allowable.
- PA 3 permits *"office equipment, computing devices, or information technology such as computers, printers, scanners… and related equipment."*
- No general grant-writing-cost prohibition was located in the CTAS text; **grant-writing allowability is UNKNOWN** for CTAS.
- Match at 34 U.S.C. § 10616 is waived for PA 5 and PA 8 grantees.

**OVW Tribal Affairs Division (TAD)** — `justice.gov/ovw/tribal-affairs`: *"TAD administers six programs that are specifically for Tribal governments, programs, and communities"* and *"Tribal entities are generally eligible to apply for any OVW grant program for which a comparable non-Tribal entity is eligible."* Verified on `justice.gov/ovw/funding-opportunities`:
- **Tribal Governments Program** — Accepting applications, closing **2026-09-03**. Eligible: *"federally recognized Tribes, Tribal consortia, or an organization that is acting as the authorized designee of a federally recognized Indian Tribe."* FY25: 64 awards / $47.47M.
- **Tribal Sexual Assault Services Program (TSASP)** — Accepting applications, closing **2026-09-03**. Eligible: *"Indian Tribes, Tribal consortia, Tribal organizations, and nonprofit Tribal organizations."* Broadest tribal-nonprofit eligibility in the DOJ set.
- **Tribal Jurisdiction Program (STCJ)** — **CLOSED** for FY26. FY25: 8 awards / $7,054,815. Eligible: tribal governments with jurisdiction over Indian country or occupying Alaska Native villages.
- **Tribal SAUSA Program** — *"Not currently accepting applications."*
- **STCJ Reimbursement Program** — separate reimbursement mechanism, own page.
- Note a **court-ordered stay on a certification requirement** flagged at the top of the OVW funding page — affects scope-of-activity restrictions. Monitor that banner.

**OVC Tribal Victim Services Set-Aside (TVSSA)** — confirmed via `ojp.gov/tribal-funding-directory` as *"Tribal Victim Services Set-Aside **Formula** Grant Program."* Verbatim allowable uses: *"community needs assessment and strategic planning; victim service programs; community outreach and education; other activities needed to address the needs of a wide variety of crime victims in tribal communities including MMIP."* Program page: `ovc.ojp.gov/program/tribal/overview` (linked, not directly fetched). Software allowability: **likely-allowable** (planning + needs assessment expressly named; software not named). **Not listed as open** on the OJP current-opportunities page as of 2026-08-26.

**OJJDP**: *"No open funding opportunities at this time"* — tribal youth work is currently routed through CTAS PA 8/PA 9.

**COPS Office** — `cops.usdoj.gov/grants` is server-rendered Drupal 10 with an HTML deadline table (program, opportunity number, both deadlines, OPEN/CLOSED flags) plus a separate **NOFO Finder** at `cops.usdoj.gov/notice-of-funding-opportunities-finder`. CTAS appears here too (`cops.usdoj.gov/ctas`). Note the site banner: *"Grants.gov searches are now powered by Simpler.Grants.gov."* COPS award-document PDFs are at predictable paths (`/pdf/2025AwardDocs/<program>/`). No COPS-specific bar on grant-writing or consultant costs was found on the pages fetched — **UNKNOWN**, not "none exists."

**Tribal Access Program (TAP)** — **This is a services program, not a grant.** DOJ acts as the CJIS Systems Agency; DOJ *"assumes responsibility for granting network access… ensures personnel, IT, and physical security; vetting and on-boarding; testing; training; and auditing."* Two tiers: TAP-LIGHT (name-based checks, person/property entry) and TAP-FULL (adds fingerprint hardware). 152 Tribes / 500+ agencies participating. FY26 application period open (`justice.gov/tribal/fy26-application`). Limited to *"selected federally-recognized Tribes."* *"Selection of Tribes for participation in the program will be limited this year based on funding availability."* No money flows to the Tribe — so no software cost allowability question; it is itself a software/data-access benefit. **High strategic relevance to NativeForge as an adjacent/complementary system, low relevance as a funding source.**

### 2. DHS / FEMA

**Critical scraping finding: fema.gov serves degraded and stale content to non-browser clients.**
- `fema.gov/grants` returned a near-empty stub whose newest item was a 2014 press release, `Last Updated: 08/04/2014`.
- `fema.gov/grants/preparedness/firefighters/assistance-grants` returned `Last updated Jul 21, 2020`.
- `fema.gov/grants/guidance-tools/fema-go` returned `Last updated Aug 3, 2020` with text describing FEMA GO as *"the new grants management system"* launched *"initially for Assistance to Firefighters Grants only."*
- `fema.gov/grants/preparedness/tribal-cybersecurity` and `fema.gov/grants/preparedness/state-local-cybersecurity` both returned **completely empty bodies**.

Program pages that *did* render (THSGP, HSGP, HMGP, BRIC) render without dates or NOFO file links resolved. **Recommendation: do not scrape fema.gov as primary. Use Grants.gov / Simpler.Grants.gov as the authoritative FEMA NOFO source and treat fema.gov as a headless-browser-rendered secondary.**

**OpenFEMA API — verified, and verified *not* to cover NOFOs.** Base path `https://www.fema.gov/api/open`, versioned (`v1`/`v2`), *"Use of the service is free and does not require a subscription or API key."* OData-style params (`$filter`, `$select`, `$orderby`, `$top` max 10,000, `$skip`, `$count`, `$allrecords`, `$format` json/jsona/csv/geojson/parquet). OpenAPI 3.0 spec at `/api/open/metadata/v3.0/OpenApi.json`. `lastRefresh` supports incremental pulls. I enumerated the full dataset list via `/api/open/v1/DataSets?$select=name&$format=csv&$top=400`. It contains **50 datasets, none of which is a funding-opportunity/NOFO dataset.** Coverage is awards, claims, and disaster outcomes: `HazardMitigationAssistanceProjects`, `HmaSubapplications`, `HmaSubapplicationsFinancialTransactions`, `EmergencyManagementPerformanceGrants`, `NonDisasterAssistanceFirefighterGrants`, `PublicAssistanceFundedProjectsSummaries`, `HazardMitigationPlanStatuses`, `DisasterDeclarationsSummaries`, NFIP series. **Verdict: OpenFEMA is excellent for competitive intelligence and eligibility signals (e.g. `HazardMitigationPlanStatuses` tells you which Tribes hold an approved mitigation plan — a hard gate on HMA project funding) and useless for opportunity discovery.**

**THSGP** — verified active as a program; **latest NOFO published is FY 2025 at $13.5M**. FY totals: FY25 $13.5M, FY24 $13.5M, FY23 $15M, FY22 $15M, FY21 $15M. No FY2026 NOFO on the page. Tribal-specific and direct (not state pass-through). Page says *"the ability of Tribal Nations to prevent, prepare for, protect against, and respond to potential terrorist attacks."* The page as rendered does **not** state the federal-recognition test, so I mark it UNKNOWN rather than assume — but note the statutory term of art is "directly eligible tribe," and state-recognized Tribes are widely understood excluded. **Flag for legal verification against the FY25 NOFO PDF before shipping eligibility logic.**

**HSGP** — three sub-programs: SHSP, UASI, OPSG. FY25 $1.008B ($373.5M SHSP / $553.5M UASI / $81M OPSG). Latest NOFO FY2025. **Tribes are named beneficiaries but not direct recipients:** SHSP funds go to State Administering Agencies, which subaward. Tribal access is therefore via 50-state SAA subrecipient competitions — a separate, high-effort monitoring surface (OJP maintains an SAA directory at `ojp.gov/funding/state-administering-agencies/overview`). **Note: EMPG is NOT part of HSGP** — the HSGP page lists only SHSP/UASI/OPSG. EMPG is a separate program; I did not verify its page, so its tribal pathway is UNKNOWN here.

**HMGP** — verified: *"provides funding to state, local, tribal and territorial governments."* Post-declaration only. **Tribal direct-recipient option confirmed** by two page statements: contact *"their appropriate state, tribe or territory (applicant level) Hazard Mitigation Office"*, and *"32 tribes are working directly with FEMA under the emergency declaration."* Hard gate, verbatim: *"All state, local, tribal and territorial governments must develop and adopt hazard mitigation plans to receive funding for hazard mitigation project application."* Planning grants are an eligible activity — *"Developing and adopting hazard mitigation plans."* Individuals/businesses cannot apply.

**BRIC — report exactly what the page says.** As fetched, `fema.gov/grants/mitigation/building-resilient-infrastructure-communities` describes BRIC in the present tense (*"BRIC supports states, local governments, tribes and territories"*), and its most recent content is **FY23 selections announced July 2, 2024** across five funding opportunities including a **Tribal Set-Aside** and a **Tribal Building Code Plus-Up**, ~$1B total, plus 93 communities in BRIC Direct Technical Assistance. **The page does not announce a current open competition, and it does not state that the program is terminated or cancelled.** I have no verified basis to call BRIC either active or terminated for FY26. **Status: UNKNOWN.** Do not put BRIC in the MVP pipeline on the assumption it is live. BRIC DTA is non-financial and notably needs no prior award and no approved mitigation plan.

**FMA** — not fetched. **UNKNOWN.** (`fema.gov/grants/mitigation/floods` is the expected path.)

**Tribal Cybersecurity Grant Program / State and Local Cybersecurity Grant Program** — both candidate URLs returned **empty bodies**. Cannot confirm active, terminated, or relocated. **Status: UNKNOWN.** Requires re-verification via Grants.gov or a rendered browser fetch before any claim is made to users.

**AFG / SAFER tribal eligibility** — the AFG page (stale 2020 render) states awards go to *"fire departments, state fire training academies and emergency medical service organizations."* Tribal fire departments are **not named** on the page as fetched. **Tribal eligibility: UNKNOWN — do not assert.** SAFER was mentioned only as a future FEMA GO integration target; not verified.

### 3. Department of Education

**No reorganization or program-transfer statement appeared on any ED page I fetched.** The Office of Indian Education page is live at `ed.gov/about/ed-offices/oese/oie` under OESE. `ed.gov/grants-and-programs` was `Page Last Reviewed: June 29, 2026`. I report the absence of such notices; I did not verify their absence department-wide.

**The forecast page has moved and been renamed.** There is no live "Forecast of Funding Opportunities" page at the old-style path; `ed.gov/grants-and-programs/apply-grant/forecast-funding-opportunities` returned empty. The functional replacement is **`ed.gov/grants-and-programs/apply-grant/available-grants`**, labeled "Funding Forecast" on the parent page, meta description *"View the grants forecast for open and upcoming grant opportunities available from ED."* It is server-rendered Drupal 10 with an HTML results table (Grant, Eligibility, Competition Status, Max Award, Expected # of Awards) and faceted filters. Facet values directly useful to NativeForge: **"Indian Tribes and Tribal Organizations," "Indian Tribe or Consortia Located on a Reservation," "Tribal Education Agencies (TEAs)," "Tribally Controlled Colleges and Universities (TCCUs)," "Minority Serving Institutions (MSIs)."** Competition Status values: All / Coming Soon / Open. This is a **search endpoint monitor** target — the facets are a real filter surface, and the page also says *"The definitive list of U.S. federal government grants is located at Grants.gov."*

**Material finding: as of 2026-08-26 the ED forecast shows only 5 open competitions, all IES research-training/methodology (84.305B, 84.324B, 84.305D, 84.305S, 84.324S). Zero Indian Education competitions are open.** Page Last Reviewed 2026-06-01.

**Title VI Indian Education Formula Grants — the distinctive rules, verified.** Applicants: LEAs, Indian Tribes, Indian Organizations, Indian Community-Based Organizations (ICBOs), and consortia of these. Requires *"the participation and written approval of an Indian Parent Committee (IPC)"* plus *"meaningful consultation and ongoing collaboration with nearby Indian Tribes."* **Student eligibility is materially broader than DOJ's federal-recognition test.** Verbatim, "Indian" means a member of an Indian tribe or band, *as membership is defined by the tribe or band*, including:
- *"Any tribe or band terminated since 1940"*
- *"Any tribe or band recognized by the State in which the tribe or band resides"*
- *"A descendant, in the first or second degree"* of such an individual
- anyone *"considered by the Secretary of Interior to be an Indian for any purpose"*
- *"An Eskimo, Aleut, or other Alaska Native"*
- a member of an organized Indian group that received an Indian Education Act 1988 grant as in effect 10/19/1994

**So: state-recognized Tribes' members COUNT for Title VI child counts, and terminated tribes and first/second-degree descendants count too.** This is the sharpest eligibility contrast in the whole DOJ/DHS/ED set and should be encoded as a distinct rule, not folded into a generic "tribal" flag. Whether a state-recognized Tribe may itself be the *applicant* is not resolved by the page — **UNKNOWN, verify against the statute/regs.** Authorized activities include Native language and restoration programs, and *"Acquisition of equipment, only if the acquisition is essential to achieve the purpose of meeting the unique cultural, language, and educational needs of Indian students"* — a narrow, purpose-tied equipment test, so software is **sometimes-allowable-depends-on-NOFO**.

**ED Native program inventory (all URLs verified on the ED landing page for Native/Alaska Native/Pacific Islander grants):** Demonstration Grants for Indian Children (DEMO); Indian Education Professional Development (PD); Native American and Alaska Native Children in School (NAM); Native American Language Grant (NAL@ED); Native American Language Resource Center (NALRC); State Tribal Education Partnership (STEP, 84.415A); Tribally Controlled Postsecondary CTE; Native American CTE Program; **American Indian Tribally Controlled Colleges and Universities (Title III-A)**; American Indian Vocational Rehabilitation Services (84.250); **Alaska Native Education (84.356A)**; **Native Hawaiian Education (84.362A)**; Alaska Native and Native Hawaiian Serving Institutions (Title III-F); **Native American-Serving Nontribal Institutions (NASNTI, Title III-F)**; AANAPISI; AAPI Data Disaggregation (D2); Native Hawaiian CTE.

DEMO and NALRC have unusually broad eligibility, verbatim: *"Indian tribe, Tribal college or university (TCU), Tribal Education Agency (TEA), Local Education Agency (LEA), BIE-funded school, Alaska Native Regional Corporation, a tribal, Alaska Native, Native Hawaiian, or other nonprofit organization, a nontribal for-profit organization may apply."* **Note: nontribal for-profits are eligible** — relevant if NativeForge ever applies directly rather than serving applicants.

**Perkins CTE tribal/BIE set-aside** — not verified. ED routes CTE to `cte.ed.gov`, which I did not fetch. The Native American CTE Program and Tribally Controlled Postsecondary CTE pages exist (URLs above) but I did not open them. **Set-aside mechanics: UNKNOWN.**

### 4. Department of Labor

**DINAP page is server-rendered Drupal 10 and carries an Announcements block that is the single best DOL monitoring target.** Verified WIOA Section 166 vision statement covers *"tribes, tribal organizations, Alaska Native entities, Indian controlled organizations and Native Hawaiian organizations serving unemployed and low income Native Americans, Alaska Natives and Native Hawaiians."*

**The multi-year designation cycle is confirmed and its current instance is closed.** **FOA-ETA-26-20**, "Program Year 26 WIOA Indian and Native American Programs – Employment and Training Grants." **Issue Date 2026-04-02, Closing Date 2026-05-22.** Full FOA PDF at `dol.gov/sites/dolgov/files/ETA/grants/2026%20PDFs/FOA-ETA-26-20.pdf`. Two distinct Grants.gov postings — applicants must pick the right one:
- **FOA-ETA-26-20-IA** — Comprehensive Services Program (adults) — grants.gov listing 361739
- **FOA-ETA-26-20-IY** — Supplemental Youth Services Program (youth) — grants.gov listing 361740

Uses: *"employment, financial assistance for education and training, and other supportive services necessary to achieve economic self-sufficiency."* Software allowability: **unclear** — nothing in the page text addresses it; the FOA PDF would need review.

DINAP also maintains `dinap/grantees`, `dinap/advisories`, `dinap/performance`, and the Native American Employment and Training Council page. ETA's Office of Grants Management catalog page is `dol.gov/agencies/eta/grants/apply/find-opportunities`. The OGM page carries the 2024 Uniform Guidance update: **de minimis indirect rate raised 10% → 15%, single audit threshold $750K → $1M, equipment threshold $5,000 → $10,000**, effective for awards from 10/1/24 — directly relevant to whether NativeForge-scale software purchases are "equipment" or "supplies."

**Apprenticeship** — ETA routes to `apprenticeship.gov` (separate domain, own nav). Not fetched. **UNKNOWN.**

**State WIOA Title I pass-through** — ETA lists WIOA Adult, Youth, and Dislocated Worker programs as separate program areas; these flow to state workforce agencies and then Local Workforce Development Boards. Tribes access these as subrecipients or partners, entirely separately from Section 166. I did not fetch the WIOA program pages, so the pass-through mechanics here are **asserted structurally, not verified** — treat as a 50-state monitoring surface to be scoped later, same as the FEMA SAA problem.

### 5. SBA

**SBA has migrated to WordPress (generator: `WordPress 7.0.2`) and URLs have shifted.** The ONAA request redirected from `/headquarters-offices/office-native-american-affairs` to **`/about-sba/sba-locations/headquarters/office-of-native-american-affairs/`**. Legacy content is parked at `legacy.sba.gov`. Expect further URL churn; canonicalize via redirects.

**ONAA** — modified 2026-07-30. Mission covers *"American Indians, Alaska Natives and Native Hawaiians seeking to create, develop and expand small businesses."* Acting Associate Administrator: **Elizabeth Habib**. **ONAA runs no grant program.** It offers *"free technical assistance… marketing, strategic and operational planning, financial analysis, opportunity development and capture, contract management, and compliance"* and points to an entrepreneurial-empowerment workshop vendor (Sister Sky, Inc.). Resources listed: 8(a), SAM, SBA Tribal Consultation Policy.

**8(a) is a certification, not funding — confirmed by site architecture.** 8(a) Business Development appears exclusively under `sba.gov/certifications/`, never under Loans/Grants. Same for HUBZone and WOSB.

**SBA grants page, verbatim and decisive:** *"SBA does not provide grants for starting and expanding a business."* *"SBA provides grants to nonprofits, Resource Partners, and educational organizations."* So for NativeForge, SBA is a **thin** source: Native-owned businesses get loans, certifications, and free counseling — not grants. The grantable surface is cooperative agreements to intermediaries:
- **SBDC** — competed only to fill Host Institution vacancies (currently Northeast California, Pennsylvania). Eligible: *"institutions of higher education and Women's Business Centers."*
- **VBOC**, SDVETP, VFPETP, WVETP, Boots to Business — veteran-focused cooperative agreements, several restricted to *"current recipient organizations only."*
- **SCORE** — restricted to current recipients.
- **Portable Assistance Program** — restricted to current SBDC Lead Centers.
- **PRIME** — the one genuinely tribe-eligible SBA grant, verbatim: *"provides funding for nonprofit microenterprise development organizations run either privately, or by state, local, tribal governments, or Indian tribes."* Competitive, annual: *"Announcements are posted to grants.gov in either April or May of each year."*
- **STEP** — awards go to state/territory governments (FY24 table published); small businesses access via their state. **No tribal-government recipient path.**
- **E2G Manufacturing in America** — *"Applications are now closed."*
- SBIR/STTR routed to `sbir.gov`; FAST Partnership to `sbir.gov/community/fast`.

**WBC (Women's Business Centers)** — not listed as its own funding opportunity on the grants page; appears only as an eligible SBDC host type. **Status of the WBC cooperative agreement competition: UNKNOWN.**

**7(j) Management and Technical Assistance** — `sba.gov/federal-contracting/contracting-assistance-programs/7j-management-and-technical-assistance-program` returned an **empty body** (URL appears dead post-migration), and 7(j) is **not mentioned anywhere** on the SBA grants page or the ONAA page. **Status: UNKNOWN.** Do not list 7(j) as a verified active program.

**APEX Accelerators current administrator — UNKNOWN.** `apexaccelerators.us` returned a title tag and empty body: it is a **client-side JS application** with no server-rendered content. No administering agency could be verified. **Do not state that SBA administers APEX; nothing I fetched supports that, and nothing I fetched contradicts it either.** Requires a rendered fetch or a DoD-side source.

### Cross-cutting monitoring recommendations
1. **Grants.gov / Simpler.Grants.gov is the single most valuable target in this whole scope** — it is the only source that reliably covers FEMA NOFOs, both DOL INAP postings, and everything ED tells you is "definitive." Two separate DOJ sites independently redirect users there. Prioritize its API above every agency page below.
2. **DOJ pages are the best-behaved scrape targets in this scope** — clean Drupal, `Date Modified` fields, stable PDF paths, no JS.
3. **FEMA is the worst** — stale/empty renders, no NOFO API. Headless browser or bust.
4. GovDelivery email bulletins verified for DOJ, OVW (topic `USDOJ_59`), COPS, and ED (`public.govdelivery.com/accounts/USDE/signup/49108`). **No RSS/Atom feed was verified anywhere in this scope.** Do not assume RSS exists.
5. Watch the **Bureau of Justice Grants consolidation** and the **OVW certification-requirement court stay** as active-change signals.
