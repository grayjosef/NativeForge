### A1. HUD — Office of Native American Programs (ONAP)

**CRITICAL SCRAPER FINDING: HUD.gov has migrated its entire information architecture.** The URL scheme given in the brief (`/program_offices/public_indian_housing/ih`) is now a **dead shell**. It returns HTTP 200 with a valid page skeleton but *zero body content*, and the `<title>` is literally prefixed `25red-` (a 2025-redesign staging artifact): `25red-Indian Housing - HUD's Office of Native American Programs (ONAP)`. Same for `/program_offices/public_indian_housing/ih/codetalk` → `25red-ONAP`. Any monitor pointed at the old paths will silently report "no change" forever. This is a false-negative trap, not a 404.

- **Live ONAP hub is `https://www.hud.gov/codetalk`.** Content-rich, renders server-side, cleanly scrapable. It is the single highest-value HUD monitoring target — it carried, on the date fetched: the FY26 IHBG/NHHBG income-limit guidance (ONAP-PG-2026-01 / -02), a new June 2026 Federal Register disaster-waiver notice for IHBG/ICDBG/NHHBG, the FY2027 IHBG formula allocation DTL, a statutory income-exclusion change from the Disabled Veterans Housing Support Act (P.L. 119-70, enacted Jan 20 2026), two radon-notice extensions (CPD-2026-006, CPD-2026-011), and event notices.
- New program URL pattern is `https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/public-indian-housing-*`. Verified live: `-ihbg`, `-ihbg-cgp`, `-grants-icdbg`. Linked-and-named but not individually fetched: `-tribal-hud-vash`, `-title-vi-loan-guarantee-program`, `-native-hawaiian-housing` (NHHBG), `-tribal-letters` (Dear Tribal Leader Letters), `-ihbgformula`, `-gems`, `-nahasda`, `-nahasda-guidance`, `-calendar`, `-training`, `-tiac`, `-tribal-consult`, `-negotiated-rule`, `-onap-reports`, `-best-practices`, `-offices`, `-grants-oversight`.

**IHBG (formula).** Verified language, and it is the most important eligibility sentence found in this entire sweep: *"Eligible IHBG recipients are federally recognized Tribes, Tribally Designated Housing Entities (TDHEs), and **a limited number of State-recognized Tribes**."* So IHBG is one of the few federal programs where state-recognized Tribes are **not** categorically excluded — but the set is closed/grandfathered, not open. Recipients must submit an Indian Housing Plan and Annual Performance Report (HUD-52737) through **GEMS**, which is now live in all ONAP regions (GEMS has replaced EPIC). Governing regs 24 CFR Part 1000, NAHASDA.

**IHBG-Competitive (IHBG-COMP).** Eligibility is narrower than IHBG: only **existing IHBG Formula recipients**. Verified deadline: *"FY 2025 IHBG-COMP NOFO: Applications Due January 15, 2026."* NOFO number FR-6900-N-43 (note: the linked PDF is named `FR-6900-N-48`, an internal inconsistency on HUD's own page — treat NOFO numbers from this page as unreliable without Grants.gov cross-check). Deadline pattern: annual, NOFO published ~Sept, due ~Jan, explicitly *"subject to annual appropriations."* Application package includes **HUD-426 "Indirect Costs Information for Award Applicant/Recipient"** and a combined Cost Summary/Implementation Schedule (HUD-4123/4125) — direct evidence that indirect and administrative cost structures are formally recognized in the award.

**ICDBG.** Two tracks. *Single Purpose* = competitive, annual NOFO. *Imminent Threat (ICDBG-IT)* = **noncompetitive, first-come-first-served, rolling** — up to $5M/yr set-aside, governed by **PIH 2025-09** (which supersedes Program Guidance 2018-04). Verified: FY25 ICDBG NOFO had a **revised** deadline of December 10, 2025 (the page flags "**New Deadline**" — deadline revision is a real event class your monitor must catch). Codetalk also stated FY26 ICDBG-IT funds remained available. Eligibility: *"any Indian tribe, band, group, or nation (including Alaska Indians, Aleut, and Eskimos) or Alaska Native village which has established a relationship to the Federal government as defined in the program regulations. In certain instances, tribal organizations may be eligible."* Federal recognition effectively required; state-recognized Tribes appear excluded. Since Jan 2026, ICDBG grantees must exclude VA service-connected disability compensation from LMI income determinations.

**ICDBG software/planning/admin allowability — strongest finding in this sweep.** 24 CFR 1003.206 (verified verbatim on eCFR, unchanged since 2017) makes data-systems and compliance-software costs **clearly allowable** within a cap. Combined §1003.205 + §1003.206 spend is capped at **20% of grant plus program income**. Directly supporting text: program administration includes *"(iii) Developing systems for assuring compliance with program requirements"*; and *"(4) Other costs for goods and services required for administration of the program, including such goods and services as rental or purchase of equipment…"*; and *"(c) Indirect costs may be charged to the ICDBG program under a cost allocation plan prepared in accordance with 2 CFR part 200, subpart E."* Most consequentially for a grant-intelligence product, **§1003.206(d)**: *"ICDBG funds may be used to prepare applications for other Federal programs where the grantee determines that such activities are necessary or appropriate to achieve its community development objectives."* That is explicit federal authority to spend ICDBG dollars on grant-seeking capacity. Separately, technical assistance to build capacity for a specific funded activity is *excluded* from the admin cap but capped at 10% of the total award.

**HUD Exchange.** Useful but **not** the ONAP notice channel the brief hypothesized. Its A–Z program index (`/programs/`) covers CDBG/HOME/CoC/ESG etc.; for tribal programs it **links out to hud.gov rather than hosting content** — ICDBG, Title VI, Tribal HUD-VASH, Section 184/184A all resolve to `hud.gov`. `https://www.hudexchange.info/programs/ihbg` returned an **empty body** (no such page). There is a genuine new tribal hub at `/programs/priority-tribal/` ("Tribal Housing: Empowering Local Choices," modified 2026-03-27) which aggregates ICDBG/IHBG/184/Title VI/Tribal HUD-VASH plus two tribal-specific resources: the *Housing and Infrastructure Development in Indian Country Planning Guide* and the *Tribal/Interagency Environmental Streamlining (TIES) Toolkit*. HUD Exchange does run a mailing list (`/mailinglist/`) and a News section (`/news/`), and BABA tribal-recipient guidance lives at `/programs/baba/tribal-recipients/`. Treat HUD Exchange as **Tier 2 secondary**; treat Codetalk as Tier 1 primary.

**Email/notice channels verified:** Codetalk listserv signup at `https://www.hud.gov/subscribe/signup?listname=What%20is%20New%20on%20Codetalk&list=CODETALK-L`; a rolling **"What's New on Codetalk" PDF** at `https://www.hud.gov/sites/dfiles/PIH/documents/Whats-New-on-Codetalk.pdf`; DTLs as PDFs under `hud.gov/sites/dfiles/PIH/documents/`; PIH notices at `hud.gov/hudclips/notices/pih` and ONAP-specific at `hud.gov/hudclips/notices/pih-native-american-programs`. The IHBG formula process runs on a **third-party domain, `ihbgformula.com`** (verified reference: `https://ihbgformula.com/fy2027/`) — a non-.gov dependency worth flagging for terms review. Verified formula deadlines: FY2027 Formula Response Form corrections due **August 1, 2026**; census challenges for FY2028 due **March 30, 2027**.

### A9. Cross-cutting scraper intelligence

1. **Two agencies renamed/moved their web presence within the last ~14 months** (HUD IA migration; FNS→FNA domain move). Build a canonical-URL resolver that follows redirects and compares `<link rel="canonical">` against your stored URL, and alert on drift. Static URL lists will rot fast in this portfolio.
2. **The dead-shell failure mode is the top risk.** HUD's old `/program_offices/` paths return 200 + valid HTML + zero content. Content-length and body-hash checks are mandatory; HTTP status is not sufficient. Add a rule: title starting with a redesign artifact prefix (e.g. `25red-`) ⇒ flag as stale, do not diff.
3. **Government-shutdown banners are page-scoped, not site-scoped.** `eda.gov/funding` carried an appropriations-lapse notice and placeholder `test` content while `eda.gov/funding/programs` was fully current. Detect "not being updated" banners per page and suppress those pages from user-facing output rather than blacklisting the domain.
